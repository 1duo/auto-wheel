AUTOWHEEL / ROBINHOOD MCP

A small wheel that prioritizes
releasing capital.

The Go daemon manages one dedicated Robinhood agentic account, with one contract per new order and at most three AutoWheel-managed symbols. It uses deterministic rules; an AI model does not choose or submit trades.

Readiness and explicit activation are required

Deployment defaults to read-only. Live trading requires authenticated readiness, release/configuration-bound native activation and verified account isolation. Same-account peer retirement requires flat lifecycles, a native account/database-bound stopped-state seal, an inactive persistently masked peer service and a lifetime operational lock. A paused peer retains its ownership; external shares are never appropriated.

The explicit exclusive_standard policy checks supported standard-order history and durable AutoWheel receipts while disclosing that advanced/OCO visibility is unknown. Keep that account exclusive to AutoWheel; manual, contingent or other-strategy orders are prohibited. Missing or ambiguous facts block execution. Coordinator observation alone grants no shared trading authority.

01 / CASH → PUT

Sell a cash-secured put

Nearest eligible weekly expiry. Conservative: absolute delta 0.05–0.15, lowest delta first. Balanced: 0.15–0.35, highest net premium per collateral dollar per calendar day first.

02 / PUT → SHARES

Reconcile assignment

Broker facts and recorded ownership must agree. A disappearance holds the slot for at least 24 hours. Ambiguity blocks new orders.

03 / SHARES → CASH

Sell an aggressive call

Earliest eligible daily or weekly expiry. Delta 0.50–0.85. Highest delta wins; time-value yield breaks ties. Below-cost strikes are allowed.

Exact selection rules

Conservative favors lower assignment risk by ranking the lowest absolute put delta first, then premium yield. Balanced accepts the wheel transition from a sold put to assigned shares to covered calls and ranks puts by premium yield. Delta is a risk proxy, not a guarantee or an exact assignment probability. Both modes use covered calls for any confirmed assigned shares. No stop-loss threshold is introduced.

Put expirationNearest broker-listed Friday, 1–8 calendar days away. A Thursday explicitly marked weekly by the broker is also eligible. Expiration eligibility comes from broker-listed chains; the reviewed session calendar gates trading sessions only. No same-day expiry.
Put rankingStrike below current underlying price and negative delta. Conservative: magnitude 0.05–0.15, lowest magnitude first. Balanced: magnitude 0.15–0.35, highest yield first. Yield is (bid × 100 − estimated fee) / (strike × 100) / calendar DTE. Conservative uses yield after delta ties. Equal yields favor lower collateral, then stable instrument ID.
Call expirationEarliest listed expiration tomorrow through 8 calendar days away. Daily expirations are allowed; same-day expirations are excluded.
Call rankingPositive delta 0.50–0.85. Within the earliest expiry, highest delta comes first. For equal delta, rank (extrinsic bid × 100 − fee) / (spot × 100) / calendar DTE. Extrinsic bid is max(bid − max(spot − strike, 0), 0). Calls need positive total net premium, but their extrinsic yield can be zero or negative after fees.
Across symbolsEligible covered calls precede all new puts. Conservative puts rank by lowest absolute delta, then daily yield; balanced puts rank by daily yield. Call allocation uses daily time-value yield. Alphabetical symbol breaks ties. It submits at most one new order in each 5-minute cycle, then reconciles before the next submission.
Illiquid nearest expirySkip that symbol. The scanner does not move to a later expiry when the nearest expiry fails liquidity or delta checks.
OwnershipCalls require exactly 100 available, AutoWheel-owned, broker-confirmed shares. External stock remains untouched and its symbols are excluded from new trades. Only AutoWheel-managed symbols consume the three wheel slots.

Capital and parallelism

Default configuration uses an $80,000 capital ceiling and $0 additional reserve. New put funding uses the lesser of broker cash and unleveraged buying power, excludes pending deposits and unsettled funds, and respects broker cash reservations. Existing commitments consume the capital ceiling: share purchase cost, short-put collateral, remaining pending-put collateral, and known missing-put commitments. For each new put, the estimated $1 fee per contract must fit both cash and capital budgets. Calls deduct that fee estimate from premium and require share coverage; the planner does not reserve additional put collateral for them. No margin or expected premium funds a new put.

Three active symbols means three possible wheel positions, not three independent accounts. Managed stock, options, pending orders, journal ownership and settlement holds count toward that limit. External stock purchase cost and short puts still consume the capital budget, and broker cash reservations remain authoritative. Three scan workers share a serialized MCP transport; capital allocation and order submission remain serialized.

Execution and checks

  • Scan every 300 seconds during reviewed US-equity exchange sessions covering 2026–2028: 09:35–15:45 New York ordinary, 09:35–12:45 early close; closed holidays and dates outside coverage stay closed. Fresh tradable broker quotes are still required. Session sources: NYSE hours/calendars · Nasdaq Trader calendar.
  • Bid at least $0.05; open interest at least 100; bid size at least 1; spread at most 20% of ask. Option and underlying quotes must be no older than 30 seconds or more than 2 seconds in the future.
  • Standard 100-share, zero-cash deliverables and active, tradable contracts only. One day-limit sell-to-open order at bid, rounded down to the broker’s price tick.
  • Re-read account holdings and cash, reselect a fresh contract, validate broker order review, and persist/fsync a unique submission reference before sending.
  • Any unknown order result retains its intent and freezes new orders. No automatic write retry, repricing, rolling, buyback, stop loss, or earnings-event filter.

How assignment and call-away are recognized

After a confirmed own put fill disappears from open options/orders, the journal starts a 24-hour hold. With exclusive control of that managed symbol, exactly 100 settled available shares at the put strike and no intervening equity order support an inferred assignment. This is labeled as an inference, not a broker assignment receipt. Other inventory requires receipt-based operator adoption.

After owned shares disappear and no open option or order remains, wait at least 24 hours before releasing the slot and considering a new put. If a covered call expires while the 100 shares remain available, the next eligible call can be selected. These conservative holds can delay capital reuse.

Below-cost calls can deliberately realize losses

Illustration, not current quotes: a $200 put sold at $2 with 4 days left requires $20,000 collateral and nets $199 after the fee estimate: 0.995% over 4 days, or 0.24875% per day. If assigned at $200, selling a $195 call at $2 yields ($195 − $200) × 100 + $199 = −$301 if called away. Including only that prior put premium makes the two-premium example −$102; taxes and any other transactions are excluded.

High delta and short expiry favor faster call-away; neither guarantees it. A stock decline can exceed all premiums. This strategy is not loss capped, and it does not optimize lifetime wheel profit.

Using the menubar

  • Control center: the compact panel matches OptionZero’s typography, spacing, dividers, switch and status dot. It shows execution mode, readiness, limits, available cash and wheel positions. Missing broker balances/occupancy remain unavailable.
  • Selection: choose Conservative or Balanced. Changing mode pauses trading, revokes activation and saves the preset, then restarts only AutoWheel. The loaded mode is verified and live trading stays paused until fresh readiness passes and you enable it. Accepted orders continue; TradePulse settings and service are unchanged.
  • Positions and Readiness: account/token readiness, last known broker snapshot, journal ownership and planned orders. Use Back to return to the control center.
  • Weekly P&L Calendar: inspect local weekly equity changes, switch between Live and Simulation, or import saved reports. Missing valuations stay unavailable; reports are never uploaded.
  • Strategy: rules drawn from the running configuration, selection order and a labeled hypothetical example.
  • Activity and Logs: unresolved submission references and the latest 80 msi daemon log lines.
  • Live Trading switch: one click enables real trading after readiness and account-isolation qualification pass. A paused or stopped strategy retains its inventory ownership. Turning the switch off revokes activation and durably creates HALT. It fences new submissions and survives service restarts. It cannot revoke an already accepted broker order or eliminate an order already in flight.
  • Resume / Scan now: read-only operations. Resume removes HALT, while scan queues a cycle. Setup, market-hour and reconciliation checks still apply. Neither clears an uncertain submission.
  • Refresh: fetches status only. No broker scan. Menubar automatically fetches status every 30 seconds and after wake.
  • Start / Restart / Stop msi: fixed SSH commands, not trade instructions. The daemon validates its release/configuration-bound activation record on restart. Deployments pause and revoke activation. Stop leaves broker orders and positions in place.
  • Quit: exits the Mac menubar. The Linux service continues. Reopen ~/Applications/AutoWheel.app.

Connection and setup

The native app uses a dedicated launchd SSH tunnel, 127.0.0.1:19841 → msi 127.0.0.1:9841. Both ends are loopback-only. No Robinhood credential or account number is returned to the Mac operator API. Controls require a native request header; browser-origin requests are rejected. This is a single-user local control boundary, not multi-user authentication.

Deployment scripts and native service controls contain installation-specific host and path settings. Review them before installation. Build and initialize the daemon, complete browser OAuth, discover and explicitly configure the Agentic account, and run read-only checks using docs/operations.md. The running daemon owns its journal lock; stop it before commands that need the same state directory.

Inspect docs/readiness.md for verification evidence and operational limits. The control center provides live activation and durable pause. It does not cancel orders, liquidate stock, edit budgets or delete unresolved intents.
